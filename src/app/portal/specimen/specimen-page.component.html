What Paints What

A field is a handful of parts, and each part is painted by one --formidable-* variable. This is the map the rest of the page builds on.

Notice

  • Colour, size and shape, and content are separate variables, so each changes without the others.
  • Every value is the one the page theme gives it right now.

Part 2 · {{ parts[1]!.title }}

The decorator puts a label, a prefix, a suffix and a hint around any field. Where each can go depends on the field's layout, so each chapter lists only the fields that honour it.

4

Label Positions

Six places a label can go, set by one input on formidableFieldLabel.

Notice

  • They differ most on an empty field: whether the label rests in the value area or floats, and whether the placeholder shows.
  • Only the horizontal layout has room over a field. The toggle, the slider and the two groups always label outside, so they are not listed.

Try

Switch between Empty and Filled.

5

Adornments

Content you project around a field: a prefix, a suffix, an adornment beside the label, and a hint.

Notice

  • The content is yours — an icon, text, a button. The library places it and insets the value.
  • The two groups stack their options inside the field, which leaves a prefix nothing to sit beside.

In Your Template

<formidable-field-decorator>
  <formidable-input-field name="amount" />
  <div formidableFieldLabel>Amount</div>
  <div formidableFieldPrefix>CHF</div>
  <div formidableFieldHint>Before tax</div>
</formidable-field-decorator>

Part 3 · {{ parts[2]!.title }}

What the user and the form do to a field, and what the field shows in return.

6

States

The same field empty, filled, readonly, disabled and invalid.

Notice

  • Readonly blocks edits and stays focusable. Disabled cannot be focused at all.
  • Invalid appears when a validator reports a message and revealOn allows it. Here a validator reports every field as required, and reveals at once.
  • Each state is a set of colour variables of its own, so a theme restyles one without the others.

Try

Hover or focus any field: both states are live.

In Your Template

<formidable-input-field [readonly]="true" />
<formidable-input-field [disabled]="true" />
Field State
7

Panels

The three fields that open a panel of their own.

Notice

  • A panel is painted by the same variables as its field.
  • It opens below its field, flips above when there is no room, and closes on a click outside it.

Try

All three open when this chapter comes into view, which a visitor could not do: each click would close the others. Click anywhere to close them.
